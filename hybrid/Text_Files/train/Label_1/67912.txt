from collections import deque
n,m,p = map(int,input().split())
e = list(map(int,input().split()))
g = [[0]*m for _ in range(n)]
cnt = [0]*p
q=deque([])
for i in range(n):
    for j in range(m):
        g[i][j]=-1
        c = input()[0]
        if c!='.':
            c=int(c)-1
            cnt[c]+=1
            g[i][j]=c
            q.append((i,j))
while q:
    i,j = q.popleft()
    for di,dj in [(0,1),(1,0),(0,-1),(-1,0)]:
        for d in range(1,e[g[i][j]]+1):
            ni,nj = i+di*d,j+dj*d
            if 0<=ni<n and 0<=nj<m and g[ni][nj]==-1:
                g[ni][nj]=g[i][j]
                cnt[g[i][j]]+=1
                q.append((ni,nj))
print(*cnt)
