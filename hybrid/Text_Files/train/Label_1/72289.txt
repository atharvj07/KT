n = int(input())
court_choices = list(map(int, input().split()))

# Bill's strategy to maximize the area is to choose the points in a way that they form a regular 2n-sided polygon.
# Since the court has already chosen points for every other edge, Bill should choose his points to maintain the polygon's symmetry.
# A simple strategy is to choose the points in the reverse order of the court's choices to maintain the symmetry.
# This guarantees the maximum area as it forms a regular polygon which has the maximum area for a given perimeter.

bill_choices = sorted(court_choices, reverse=True)

print(*bill_choices)
