import math
n=int(input())
lairs=[list(map(int,input().split())) for i in range (n)]
lairs.sort(key=lambda x:x[1])
if lairs[0][1]<0 or lairs[-1][1]>0:
    print(-1)
else:
    sorted_x=[x for x,y in lairs]
    furthest_left,furthest_right=sorted_x[0],sorted_x[-1]
    diameter=furthest_right-furthest_left
    radius=diameter/2
    print(radius)
